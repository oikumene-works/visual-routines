# Code Of Conduct

## Expected Conduct

Project participation should be respectful, patient, specific, and focused on
the work. Welcome questions asked in good faith, make accessibility needs easy
to state, credit other people's contributions, and disagree with ideas without
attacking people.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, publication of private information, sustained personal
attacks, and disruption intended to make participation unsafe are not
acceptable.

Do not pressure anyone to disclose a diagnosis, disability, identity, private
routine, image, or support need. Visual Routines is not a medical or emergency
service, and community participation must not be used to diagnose or direct a
person's care.

## Scope And Moderation

This code applies to project-managed repository interactions and spaces where
someone is representing the project. Repository administrators may edit,
hide, lock, or remove material and restrict participation when needed to
protect the project or its participants.

The project has no confidential conduct-reporting inbox and promises no
response time. Do not put private evidence or personal data in a public issue.
Use GitHub's platform reporting and blocking tools for abuse or immediate
platform-safety concerns. This project cannot provide emergency assistance.

Moderation authority does not imply an ongoing maintenance or support
commitment. See [MAINTAINERSHIP.md](MAINTAINERSHIP.md).
