# Visual Routines Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-30

## Scope

This policy describes Visual Routines version 0.2.0 and how the app works now.

## Data Stored On Your Device

Visual Routines stores the routines you create, including routine names,
steps, notes, order, selected image references, app-private copies of images
you select, and image descriptions. It also stores active routine progress and
local display settings. Routine text and selected images may contain personal
information if you choose to enter it. Selected images are copied for offline
use without changing the original files. Source metadata is not kept in the
normalized copies. Temporary import and recovery files may be created while
editing or saving.

## Collection And Sharing

The app does not transmit your routine data, image copies, progress, or settings
to the Visual Routines project, advertisers, analytics providers, or another
developer-operated service. The current app has no app account, backend,
analytics, advertising, tracking, or Internet permission, and it integrates no
third-party data-collection service.

## Device-To-Device Transfer

Cloud backup is disabled. On supported Android versions, Android's device
migration can transfer routines, stored image copies, active progress, and settings to another
device during migration. Android or the device migration service handles that
transfer; the app does not send the data to the Visual Routines project.

## Retention And Deletion

A routine remains on the device until you delete it in the app, clear the
app's storage, or uninstall the app. Active progress remains until you complete
or reset it, replace it after confirmation, clear app storage, or uninstall.
Unused image copies are removed after successful changes or abandoned editing.
Copies needed by a saved routine or a restorable draft are retained. Clearing
app storage or uninstalling also removes these copies. Original images outside
the app are unaffected.

Settings remain until you change them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Data
already transferred to another device must be removed separately on that
device.

## Local Security

Android's app sandbox protects the app-private files from ordinary access by
other apps. Visual Routines does not add its own encryption, so do not use
routine text as a secure vault for confidential information.

## Future Changes

This policy covers the current local-only app. If a future release adds
accounts, synchronization, remote routine management, or other network or data
processing, its maintainer must update this policy and the relevant user
disclosures before enabling or releasing that feature. This policy does not
describe or authorize features that are absent from the current version.

## Project Contact

For general feedback, email <visual-routines@oikumene.works>. **Send feedback**
opens a draft in your email app; you choose whether to send it. Visual Routines
does not attach routine content, images, logs, or device identifiers. Sending
email shares your sender address and message with the maintainer and your email
providers. Please leave out personal or sensitive information. Replies are not
guaranteed. This is not an emergency or security service.

Project information and the private vulnerability-reporting route are at
<https://github.com/oikumene-works/visual-routines>.
